Come share the joy

Help us bring a little Hallmark Christmas magic to our parish Christmas tree lot! Mary’s Women of Joy will be sharing warm beverages, friendly smiles, and Christmas cheer with families shopping for their trees. Whether you can serve refreshments or help purchase supplies, we’d love to have you join us.

Our hospitality station is open 4:00–7:00 p.m. (Eastern) Friday, Saturday and Sunday before and after Thanksgiving 2026.

☕ Beverage hosts

What does a beverage host do?

Welcome families, serve coffee, hot tea, hot chocolate and warm apple cider, offer cream, flavored syrups and other extras, keep the station tidy and inviting, and share a little Christmas cheer.

🛍️ Help us stock the Christmas cheer!

We need two women per weekend to buy the beverages and supplies. You don’t need to be at the tree lot in the evening.

All supplies are fully reimbursed by the Men’s Club. No personal donation is expected. Please save your receipts.
Shopping list

Hot beverages: regular and decaf coffee, hot chocolate, hot apple cider, assorted hot teas.
Accompaniments: half-and-half or creamer, flavored coffee syrups, sugar and substitutes, honey, mini marshmallows, whipped cream, cinnamon sticks.
Serving supplies: insulated hot cups, lids and sleeves, napkins, stir sticks, and anything else the coordinator identifies. Shoppers will receive a list with quantities.
